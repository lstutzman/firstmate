--- conclusion on the leftover check ---
The single FAIL above ("no hung fake ssh left behind (pids: 90312)") did not reproduce: three further 1s hangs sampled the process
table at +0s, +0.5s and +2s after the resolver returned and found no process matching the fake ssh. The resolver's own capture
of the read ($(...)) had already returned, which it cannot do while any process in the bounded group still holds the pipe, so the
pid seen once was a KILLed child awaiting reaping (its parent, the perl watchdog, had exited), not a live process. Verdict: bound fires, nothing survives it.
